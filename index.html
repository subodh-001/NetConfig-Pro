How does remote configuration work?

All you need is a laptop or PC plugged into your router or switch with an Ethernet cable. Download UltraViewer (recommended) or AnyDesk and share your ID & password. I connect to your screen, open WinBox (for MikroTik) or the UniFi/Cisco Web GUI, and configure everything while you watch live.

Is it safe? Can you access my router after the session?

100% safe. You see every mouse movement and command on your monitor in real-time. Once the setup is completed, you immediately change the router admin password, and UltraViewer is closed. No one can access your network without your explicit permission.

Do I need to pay anything in advance?

No! Zero advance payment. You pay ONLY after the configuration is finished, speed tests are verified, auto-failover is tested live in front of you, and you are 100% satisfied. We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Bank Transfer, or PayPal.

Every setup includes 7 days of complimentary follow-up support. If your ISP changes IP parameters or you need fine-tuning, I connect back and resolve it free of charge. You also receive exported backup files (.backup / .rsc) saved directly on your computer.
